🧵 Catch-Up Summariser

When a group chat gets really busy, this posts a short summary once things calm down, so anyone who missed it can catch up.

In the group

  • !catchup summarises the last 3 hours (!catchup 6h, !catchup 30m, up to 24 hours)
  • !catchup off / !catchup on stops or starts automatic summaries
  • React ❌ to any catch-up message to keep your messages out of summaries, ✅ to come back in
  • !catchup help explains it all again

Privacy and fairness

  • Only when messages are sent is stored, to spot busy periods. What people write is never stored.
  • To make a summary, the busy period's messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude with phone numbers and emails removed. Only the summary is posted.
  • Views are never tied to names, disagreements are described evenly, and names are only used for who's doing what.
  • Every summary says it's AI-made and shows message times, so people can check the original wording.
